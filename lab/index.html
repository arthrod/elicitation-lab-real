/ The whole point

A static form has to guess every field before the first user arrives. This one doesn't.

Elicitation is where a tool stops and asks a human. The old constraint was that someone had to author the questions up front — so intakes grew to forty fields to cover every case, and still had an Other box that collected nothing useful.

Three things fall out of generating the questions instead: you never enumerate the fields, the form gets shorter and pre-filled because the facts are already in the request, and the answer can be rendered as something that isn't a form at all.
